# 9. Tools & Resources

A curated list. Not exhaustive — just what actually gets used.

## Brokers (India)

| Broker | Strengths | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| **Zerodha** | Largest, stable Kite platform, great APIs (Kite Connect) | ₹20/trade flat |
| **Upstox** | Cheap, decent app, public API | ₹20/trade flat |
| **Angel One** | Free delivery, SmartAPI | Older UX |
| **Groww** | Beginner-friendly, mutual funds + stocks | API recently launched |
| **Dhan** | Modern UI, free intraday | Newer player |
| **ICICI Direct / HDFC Sec** | Bank-linked, 3-in-1 accounts | Higher brokerage |

**Pick on:** API quality (if you'll automate), reliability (uptime during volatile sessions), charges, and customer support.

## Charting & Analysis

- **TradingView** — gold standard. Free tier is enough to start; paid for multi-chart and alerts.
- **Chartink** — screener with custom technical scans (free).
- **Investing.com** — economic calendar, news.
- **Trendlyne** — fundamentals + screeners (India-focused).
- **Screener.in** — fundamentals deep dive (free, excellent for investing).
- **NSE / BSE official sites** — corporate announcements, FII/DII flows, derivatives data.
- **Sensibull** — options strategy builder.

## Data & APIs (for builders)

- **Kite Connect** (Zerodha) — most popular, ₹2,000/mo.
- **Upstox API** — free.
- **Angel One SmartAPI** — free.
- **NSE Python (`nsepython`)** — unofficial scraper for NSE data.
- **Yahoo Finance** — free historical data, often used for backtests.

## Books — read in this order

### Foundation
1. **"The Intelligent Investor"** — Benjamin Graham. Investing classic. Skip to chapters 8 & 20.
2. **"One Up On Wall Street"** — Peter Lynch. Practical fundamental investing.
3. **"A Random Walk Down Wall Street"** — Burton Malkiel. Why most active management fails.

### Trading & Technicals
4. **"Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets"** — John Murphy. The technical bible.
5. **"How to Make Money in Stocks"** — William O'Neil. CANSLIM method. Great for breakout traders.
6. **"Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard"** — Mark Minervini. Modern momentum trading.

### Psychology & Risk
7. **"Trading in the Zone"** — Mark Douglas. The mindset book.
8. **"Reminiscences of a Stock Operator"** — Edwin Lefèvre (1923). Timeless story of Jesse Livermore.
9. **"The Disciplined Trader"** — Mark Douglas.
10. **"Fooled by Randomness"** — Nassim Taleb. Probabilistic thinking.

### India-specific
11. **"Coffee Can Investing"** — Saurabh Mukherjea.
12. **Varsity by Zerodha** ([zerodha.com/varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity)) — **free**, comprehensive, India-focused. **Start here if you read nothing else.**

## YouTube channels worth your time

- **Zerodha Varsity** — companion videos to the website.
- **P R Sundar** — options education (India).
- **Pranjal Kamra / Finology** — investing fundamentals (India).
- **Rayner Teo** — trend following / risk management.
- **Adam Khoo** — investing & trading frameworks.

> Filter aggressively. 95% of "trading gurus" on social media are selling courses, not making money trading. Red flags: screenshots of P&L without verified records, "100% win rate," paid Telegram tip groups.

## Communities

- **r/IndianStreetBets**, **r/IndianStockMarket** — caveat emptor; entertainment + occasional gold.
- **TradingView ideas** — public charts from other traders.
- **Twitter / X** — follow practitioners, mute hype accounts.

## Practice & paper trading

- **TradingView paper trading** — built-in, realistic.
- **Sensibull** — options paper trades.
- **Moneybhai** by Moneycontrol — simulated portfolio.

## Useful command-line / dev tools

- **TA-Lib** / **pandas-ta** — Python technical indicator libraries.
- **Backtrader**, **Zipline**, **VectorBT** — Python backtesting frameworks.
- **Jupyter** — for data exploration and strategy research.
- **DuckDB / SQLite** — light, fast for storing OHLC data locally.

## Your 30-day starter plan

| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **1** | Read chapters 1–4 of this guide + Varsity Module 1. Open a demat account. |
| **2** | Read chapters 5–6 + Varsity Module 2. Set up TradingView. Watch (don't trade) 1–2 stocks daily. |
| **3** | Read chapters 7–8. Start paper trading 1 strategy. Keep a journal. |
| **4** | Review your paper trades. Identify mistakes. Refine rules. **Don't go live yet.** |

Then: paper trade for **2 more months** before risking real capital. Start with **10% of your intended capital**. Scale only after 3 profitable months.

## Final words

Trading is a long game played by patient people with strict rules. There are no shortcuts, no holy grails, and no guru worth your money.

Read. Backtest. Paper trade. Journal. Survive. Compound.
